Autism Spectrum Self-Reflection Quiz

A practical, non-diagnostic check-in for autistic traits, support needs, sensory load, masking, and executive-function friction. Think of it as a mirror, not a medical verdict.

Important: Autism Level 1, 2, and 3 are clinical support-need categories from diagnostic practice. This page cannot diagnose autism, assign a real support level, replace a clinician, or resurrect the old Asperger's label as a diagnosis. It can help you organize what to discuss with a professional or understand yourself better.
Level 1-ish: lower outside support, but effort/masking may still be enormous.
Level 2-ish: noticeable daily support needs across communication, flexibility, or sensory regulation.
Level 3-ish: very substantial support needs, especially around communication, safety, daily living, or overwhelm.

For each statement, choose what has been most true across your life, not just during one bad week.
